# Research snapshot graph schema 0.2.0

The strict dataset.schema.json is normative for shape validation. Documentary assertions, reported results and research classifications remain separate. Legal validity, evidence observation, source retrieval and curation supersession are independent dates. Curation never changes legal valid_from/valid_to. Source identities group resource URLs; observation IDs retain access, version and retrieval limits.

The portable graph wrapper uses schema 1.1.0; metadata.schema_version is the canonical schema and metadata.version is the evidence edition. Historical assertions remain available, while active edges exclude superseded claims.

Publication state is separate from evidence review. The research_seed status retains graph wrapper 1.1.0, qualified assertions, supersession history and zero independently human-reviewed assertions.
