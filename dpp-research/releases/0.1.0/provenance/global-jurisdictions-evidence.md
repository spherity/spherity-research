# Non-EU DPP regulation and ecosystems: evidence register

Research cut-off and access date: **23 October 2026**. Targeted public-source review; not an exhaustive legal survey. European Union and UK are covered by other workstreams. Translations and classification decisions below are research interpretations. A ministry's description of another jurisdiction's rules is evidence of its own engagement, not a substitute for the other jurisdiction's legislation.

## Classification rule

Keep separate: (1) enacted passport framework; (2) product-specific passport obligation and its application date; (3) legally required identification/traceability or digital labelling that is adjacent to a DPP; (4) draft standard or legislative proposal; (5) funded pilot, procurement, operating ecosystem, or government guidance. The absence of a mandate in these sources means **no mandate identified in this review**, not proven absence of law. Do not assign a whole country a single maturity score that hides simultaneous instruments.

## China — statutory battery identity and traceability; a separate DPP standard project

**G-CN-01 — enacted sectoral identity/traceability regime, digital-ID enabling provision.** *Interim Measures for the Recycling and Comprehensive Utilization of Waste Power Batteries of New Energy Vehicles* (新能源汽车废旧动力电池回收和综合利用管理暂行办法), six ministries' **Order No. 73**, promulgated **31 December 2025**, effective **1 April 2026**, official publication 16 January 2026. Article 4 establishes a national battery traceability information platform; Articles 9–10 require unique battery coding and physical marking, with GB/T 34014 incorporated by reference. Article 12 establishes a digital-identity system, but explicitly leaves detailed measures to separate provisions. Article 24 requires lifecycle event reporting by vehicle makers/importers, battery businesses, swap operators, repairers and recyclers. Article 32 supplies sanctions, including for failure to implement the digital-ID system. This is not evidence that a complete EU-equivalent passport platform was operational on the effective date. [Primary law, Articles 4, 9–12, 24–26, 32 and 50](https://www.miit.gov.cn/gyhxxhb/jgsj/cyzcyfgs/bmgz/jdcjxl/art/2026/art_392462fdc40c415ea4a4129cac3028c2.html).

**G-CN-02 — official implementation explanation.** MIIT's January 2026 press conference describes a unique, dynamic identity for each battery pack connecting manufacture, installation/sale, swapping, repair, vehicle scrapping and recycling. It also describes continuing research on the precise information scope. This supports treating statutory establishment and implementation readiness as different observations. [MIIT press conference, questions on traceability and digital ID](https://www.miit.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfbh/bxwfbh/art/2026/art_c5c9031e409e468eb4004d82fd394241.html).

**G-CN-03 — recommended national-standard project, not a published mandatory national DPP law.** Official SAMR project **20251568-T-469**, *Digital Product Passport (DPP)—General requirements* (产品数字护照（DPP）通用要求), is labelled 国家标准项目 / 制定 / 推荐性: national-standard project, new development, recommended. The record shows 3 June 2025 and an 18-month project period; initial publicity ran 13 January–12 February 2025. TC267 is responsible, with the national electronic-business standardization committee jointly involved; China Article Numbering Center is lead drafter. Scope includes identifiers, carriers, data, access/sharing, storage, security and authentication. Retain the project identifier; do not fabricate a final GB/T number or publication date. [Official project register, basic information and technical scope](https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=1CB92886179AEDB9E06397BE0A0A064B).

**G-CN-04 — ecosystem institution.** SAMR announced formation of national DPP working group **SAC/SWG 41** on **27 March 2026**, hosted by the China Article Numbering Center. It covers batteries, textiles and consumer electronics and is tasked with developing the national DPP standards system. This is an institution/programme milestone, not enactment of a passport obligation. [SAMR announcement](https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/sj/art/2026/art_8d62ff552dfc410b9fa0f594598f8e1d.html).

**Gap:** locate any subsequent Article 12 implementing measures and final national general-requirements standard before publication of the paper. The primary records reviewed do not establish these.

## Vietnam — important enacted DPP enabling framework

**G-VN-01 — legislation in force, with voluntary uptake and future product rollout.** Law **78/2025/QH15**, adopted **18 June 2025**, effective **1 January 2026**, amends the Law on Product and Goods Quality and expressly recognizes digital product passports. The consolidated official law includes the new technology provisions in Article 6đ. [Government consolidated law, 156/VBHN-VPQH, September 2025](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2025/9/156-vbhn-vpqh.pdf); [National standards authority explanation, 27 March 2026](https://tcvn.gov.vn/5-diem-moi-dinh-hinh-quan-ly-chat-luong-hang-hoa-thoi-ky-so/27/03/2026/).

**G-VN-02 — Decree 37/2026/NĐ-CP**, issued and effective **23 January 2026**. MOST's account specifies minimum passport information including product/GTIN identity, operator identities, origin, traceability, certificates where relevant, dates and safety warnings; electronic records and unique identifiers; truthful, timely updates and connectivity with the national traceability portal. A passport can substitute for an electronic label if it contains the legally required label information. Crucially, MOST says businesses are encouraged to use passports and that a product-specific implementation roadmap is to be submitted to the Prime Minister. Classify as **enacted enabling framework / conditional duties when used**, not a verified blanket DPP mandate. [MOST explanation, section “Triển khai hộ chiếu số của sản phẩm”](https://mst.gov.vn/nghi-dinh-37-ung-dung-cong-nghe-so-trong-quan-ly-chat-luong-va-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-197260812204117614.htm); [Official Gazette entry confirming issue/effective dates](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban-dang-cong-bao/chinh-phu-c1/trang-45.htm).

**Gap:** official decree download timed out; paper should preserve and check Articles 31–34 directly and find any subsequently approved mandatory product roadmap. The MOST page displays 24 January 2026 but its URL contains an August timestamp, so preserve both displayed date and retrieval date.

## Japan — government-backed operating data ecosystem and DPP demonstrations

**G-JP-01 — Ouranos industrial-data ecosystem.** METI's programme page records the ODS-RAM reference architecture whitepaper of **28 February 2025**, supply-chain guidelines for battery carbon footprints and due diligence, open-source assets, data-sharing contract templates and international interoperability activity. The page also lists 2025–2026 development/demonstration work. These are infrastructure and implementation programmes; the reviewed source does not create a domestic general DPP mandate. [METI Ouranos programme, guidelines, OSS and past-news sections](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/mono_info_service/digital_architecture/ouranos.html).

**G-JP-02 — actual cross-border demonstration, not universal interoperability certification.** NEDO announced on **31 March 2025** that Japan's Battery Traceability Platform and Catena-X had demonstrated reciprocal product carbon-footprint exchange. Work ran July 2024–January 2025. An intermediate layer reconciled authentication, protocols and data models. The platform had begun service on 16 May 2024. Model this as `demonstrated_interoperability_with`, qualified by use case and environment, rather than `fully_interoperable_with`. [NEDO announcement, demonstration table and notes 5–7](https://www.nedo.go.jp/news/press/AA5_101831.html).

**G-JP-03 — PLA-NETJ plastics DPP R&D and verification tests.** ERCA's SIP project **A1-01**, led by NEC's Akihiro Musa, explicitly describes Plastic Networking for Environmental Transformation Japan as a DPP system. Its data includes extraction/processing sites, manufacturing, CO2 and recycled-content ratios; tests trace recycled polypropylene into automotive parts. Blockchain, electronic seals and secure computation are listed as technical choices. Classification: government-funded R&D/pilot, not law. [ERCA project record, R&D overview](https://www.erca.go.jp/sip/english/overview/a101-2024.html).

**G-JP-04 — photovoltaic DPP demonstration.** Japan's Environment Ministry lists FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 demonstration work testing DPP benefits for longer solar-panel use, circularity and hazardous-substance management. This adds solar PV to the Japan map beyond batteries/plastics. [MOE programme results, relevant annual project lists](https://www.env.go.jp/recycle/circul/achievement.html).

**Gap:** architecture/industrial programme status must not be generalized into mandatory national passport coverage.

## Republic of Korea — enacted battery ID/history duties and separate passport pilots

**G-KR-01 — enacted, adjacent battery identification and safety regime.** MOLIT announced full implementation of battery safety certification and lifecycle-history requirements under the amended **Motor Vehicle Management Act (자동차관리법)** on **17 February 2025**. Individual batteries receive identifiers registered in vehicle records; replacement identifiers must be updated. The agency says a comprehensive battery-history system is planned by **2027**. Therefore 2025 identification duties and 2027 system completion are distinct events; avoid labelling this an implemented EU-style DPP mandate. [MOLIT policy announcement, paragraphs on identification, replacements and 2027 system](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148939669).

**G-KR-02 — passport platform pilot.** Busan's **7 August 2025** announcement records selection for the Ministry of Science and ICT/KISA public blockchain programme: battery-passport platform and demonstrations of charging/safety, performance checks and history management, with KRW 3.46 billion for that year. Classification: funded regional pilot; scope is not every Korean battery. [Busan government press release, subtitle and attachments](https://www.busan.go.kr/nbtnewsBU/1692400?curPage=207&srchBeginDt=&srchEndDt=&srchKey=&srchText=).

**G-KR-03 — later disclosure proposal.** MOLIT's **23 March 2026** notice proposes expanding disclosed battery information to ten categories and tougher sanctions; consultation on amendments to implementing/registration rules ran to **4 May 2026**. The retrieved announcement proves a proposal, not its final adoption. [Official notice](https://www.korea.kr/news/policyNewsView.do?newsId=148961279&pWise=main&pWiseMain=R5).

**Gap:** retrieve consolidated Korean Act and final implementing rules, including outcomes of the 2026 consultation; primary policy announcement supports the 2025 effective date but this review did not independently establish the exact amending Act number.

## India — draft Battery Pack Aadhaar system, not a verified operative obligation

**G-IN-01 — official draft guidelines, 30 December 2025.** MoRTH's *Guidelines for Implementation of Battery Pack Aadhaar System* is marked **Draft**. Section 7 proposes a unique **21-character BPAN**, alphanumeric and QR marking, and producer/importer data-upload responsibility. Section 16 proposes progression through the Automotive Industry Standards Committee/AIS process, prioritizing EV batteries and recommending later applicability to industrial batteries above 2 kWh. Do not turn the draft's “shall” language into an operative requirement or invent an implementation date. [MoRTH draft, sections 7 and 16](https://morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/Battery%20Pack%20Aadhaar%20Guideline_30122025_0.pdf); [alternate official copy, section 16 p.25](https://www.morth.gov.in/sites/default/files/Battery%20Pack%20Aadhaar%20Guideline_30122025.pdf).

**G-IN-02 — emerging standard identifier requires official verification.** TERI's **1 October 2026** project page describes research with Mercedes-Benz Research and Development India and explicitly refers to **draft AIS-227** for BPAN. This is primary evidence of TERI's research and its reference to a draft; the official draft AIS itself was not retrieved. [TERI project description](https://www.teriin.org/project/battery-lifecycle-aadhaar-enabling-digitalisation-circular-economy-and-responsible-battery).

**Gap:** MoRTH PDFs were search-index readable but direct extraction was blocked/timed out. Obtain authoritative AIS-227 text, adoption status and any incorporation into binding motor-vehicle rules before asserting a mandate.

## Canada and G7 — procurement plus an explicit UNTP policy link

**G-CA-01 — procurement/innovation challenge, not market regulation.** ISED's *Supply chain verifiable digital credentials* challenge, sponsored by Shared Services Canada and Treasury Board Secretariat, opened **15 August 2023**, closed **26 September 2023**, and was last modified **26 August 2025**. It seeks verifiable credentials and DPPs for end-to-end supply-chain transparency. Mandatory procurement outcomes reference W3C DIDs, VCs, JSON-LD and wallet/agent implementations and require at least two problem domains. These requirements bind the proposed funded solution, not all Canadian products. [ISED challenge, problem statement and essential outcomes 6, 10–11](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en/supply-chain-verifiable-digital-credentials).

**G-INT-01 — non-binding multilateral policy commitment.** G7 *Roadmap to Promote Standards-based Markets for Critical Minerals*, **31 October 2025**, builds on the 17 June 2025 action plan. Commitment 1 calls for a global interoperability framework for digital credentials and DPP compatibility aligned with **UNTP**, pilot-study results and data governance. This is a valuable graph edge connecting geopolitics, critical-mineral markets and technical interoperability; it is not itself a national passport obligation. [Natural Resources Canada official statement, commitment 1](https://www.canada.ca/en/natural-resources-canada/news/2025/10/roadmap-to-promote-standards-based-markets-for-critical-minerals.html).

**Gap:** determine procurement awards, deployments and later G7 deliverables; the challenge page alone establishes neither a national production platform nor general legislation.

## United States — federal research; adjacent sectoral records and battery labels

**G-US-01 — NIST research, completed.** NIST's *EV Battery Passport* project studies barriers and opportunities for battery passports supporting material traceability and circularity. Page created **9 May 2023**, updated **26 March 2025**, explicitly **Project Status: Completed**. Do not call it an ongoing federal implementation programme or law. [NIST project record, description, dates and status](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/ev-battery-passport).

**G-US-02 — California SB615 signed, adjacent labelling regime.** The governor's **20 September 2026** release confirms **SB615 (Allen), Vehicle traction batteries**, was signed. [Governor's official release](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/20/governor-gavin-newsom-signs-legislation-to-accelerate-californias-ev-future-expand-consumer-choice-and-strengthen-energy-independence/). The official legislative page returned 403 in this review: [official bill record](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB615). A secondary mirror of the legislative text gives Chapter 351, Statutes of 2026; HSC 44480.1 requires permanent battery labels from **1 July 2028**, permits QR-linked repositories for specified fields, and requires relabelling after remanufacture. Preserve these details as **pending direct official-text verification**, not the main source-backed mandate claim. [Text mirror](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/SB615/id/3458179). Classification: enacted battery-information law, not a comprehensive DPP.

**G-US-03 — adjacent food traceability law, not DPP.** FDA's FSMA 204 rule requires covered food businesses to retain key data elements associated with critical tracking events and supply requested information. The current official page distinguishes a proposed extension to **20 July 2028** from Congress's direction not to enforce before that date. Avoid carrying forward January 2026 as an unqualified enforcement deadline or treating these records as product passports. [FDA rule overview, compliance-date section](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-requirements-additional-traceability-records-certain-foods).

**Review conclusion:** no general US federal DPP mandate identified in the examined sources; this is a bounded finding, not a claim that all US product-data laws were surveyed.

## Australia — policy investigation and explicit discussion of digital product information

**G-AU-01 — final inquiry, not enacted obligation.** Productivity Commission report **No. 107**, delivered **22 August 2025**, publicly released **16 January 2026**, tabled **23 January 2026**, recommends investigation of a national EV-battery stewardship scheme (Recommendation 5.2). Its textile discussion, printed p. 81 footnote 4, discusses DPP/digital-labelling benefits and implementation barriers. This supersedes reliance on the March 2025 interim report. [Official report landing page](https://www.pc.gov.au/inquiries-and-research/circular-economy/report/); [Final report, Recommendation 5.2 and p. 81](https://assets.pc.gov.au/2025-10/circular-economy.pdf?VersionId=kzNYni.c_7M8UjMSOx3bhMmrRnJ.fWXa).

**G-AU-02 — government response recognizing DPP potential.** The government's response to the *Food for Thought* report recognizes QR codes, verified credentials and DPPs as potential mechanisms for expanded consumer information; FSANZ is exploring digital food-label information. This is government policy exploration and adjacent information infrastructure, not an obligation to issue DPPs. [Department of Industry response, digital-technology discussion](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/australian-government-response-food-thought-report).

**Gap:** final government response/implementation of the circular-economy inquiry and any later battery product-stewardship legislation. No general DPP mandate identified in this review.

## Singapore — exporter readiness evidence, avoid conflating TradeTrust with DPP regulation

**G-SG-01 — official business guidance.** IMDA's *Digital Technologies for Sustainability Playbook* discusses EU DPP/ESPR requirements as international market-access drivers for Singapore enterprises. It does not establish a Singapore DPP mandate. Classify the verified evidence as official readiness guidance; additional national DPP pilot claims require their own project sources. [IMDA playbook, “Meet requirements to access new markets”](https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/about/research-and-statictics/enterprise-digital-transformation/imda-digital-technologies-for-sustainability-playbook.pdf).

**Gap:** no source verified in this review for a government-mandated national Singapore DPP. TradeTrust, electronic bills of lading and green-data exchanges are relevant enabling infrastructure but must be labelled separately unless a specific DPP connection is evidenced.

## Brazil — government DPP study and a regional vehicle-passport pilot

**G-BR-01 — adopted government action plan, not product obligation.** **CONMETRO Resolution 2 of 29 May 2025**, published **1 July 2025**, adopts the **ENIQ 2025–2026 action plan**. The plan contains an Axis 3 deliverable, *Benchmarking do Passaporte Digital do Produto*, an MDIC study of DPP development. The indexed official PDF confirms the row; opening it redirected to login. [Official Gazette issue, p.51 establishing plan](https://pesquisa.in.gov.br/imprensa/servlet/INPDFViewer?captchafield=firstAccess&data=01%2F07%2F2025&jornal=515&pagina=51); [MDIC official plan PDF, indexed annex row](https://www.gov.br/mdic/pt-br/assuntos/infraestrutura-da-qualidade/conhecendo%20a%20eniq/documentos-base/resolucao-conmetro-02-2025-1.pdf). An earlier government report explicitly described seeking input for a DPP pilot through the ENIQ consultation. [AgênciaGov, August 2024, “Passaporte digital”](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202408/combate-a-pirataria-e-protecao-da-propriedade-intelectual-e-prioridade-no-governo).

**G-BR-02 — subnational vehicle-passport pilot.** Detran-PR and Tecpar-PR announced an open **120-day** pilot in **February 2026**, creating blockchain-linked vehicle histories, with ambition for statewide deployment by end 2026. This is a vehicle identity/history system, not evidence of a national environmental DPP regulation; planned rollout must remain planned absent later completion evidence. [Paraná transport department, 12 February 2026](https://www.detran.pr.gov.br/Noticia/Passaporte-digital-motoristas-do-Parana-agora-podem-ter-registro-inovador-de-veiculos).

## Africa — concrete Egypt procurement and regional policy input

**G-EG-01 — UNIDO textile-SME pilot procurement.** UNIDO RFQ **7000008382**, *Provision of Digital Product Passport (DPP) Pilot Implementation Services for Textile SMEs in Egypt*, published **17 March 2026**, closed **1 April 2026**; beneficiary Egypt. This is strong evidence of funded implementation intent. A tender does not establish contract award, completion or national mandate. [UN Global Marketplace notice 294163](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/294163).

**G-AFR-01 — regional policy consultation, not an African continental passport law.** One Planet Network reports an ITU-hosted meeting in **Malabo, 21–22 May 2026**, with officials/experts from Côte d'Ivoire, DRC, Uganda, Gabon, Ghana, Equatorial Guinea, Nigeria, South Africa, Cameroon, Kenya, Tanzania and Zimbabwe contributing to global DPIS principles. This belongs on the ecosystem/governance layer and must not colour every participant as having a DPP mandate. [One Planet Network, 24 June 2026](https://www.oneplanetnetwork.org/news-and-events/news/african-countries-shape-future-digital-product-information-systems).

**G-INT-02 — development/readiness evidence.** UNIDO's textile readiness assessment provides a useful Global South capacity baseline; its preliminary study involved 104 companies in Bangladesh, Egypt and India plus 9 associations and evaluated policy knowledge, technology, organization, supply-chain and information readiness. [UNIDO study announcement](https://hub.unido.org/news/global-south-ready-eus-digital-product-passport-first-insights-textile-sector); [full report, December 2025](https://www.unido.org/sites/default/files/unido-publications/2025-12/DPP-Report-V_1.6-lr.pdf).

**Warning:** search results for “Africa DPP Registry” describe a private platform founded by a US company. Do not infer AU/government status from a domain or the word “registry”. No governmental endorsement was verified in this review.

## Implications for knowledge-graph and map design

1. Model **instrument/project** as the unit of evidence; connect it to jurisdiction, authority, sector, lifecycle scope, actors, identifiers, dates and evidence. China, Korea and Vietnam require multiple status assertions, not one country colour.
2. Separate `adopted_on`, `entered_into_force_on`, `applies_from`, `consultation_closed_on`, `announced_on`, `expected_completion`, and `observed_on`. A 2027 government target is not a 2027 statutory compliance date.
3. Distinguish origin jurisdiction, market of sale, implementation location, funding body and cross-border partners. A Japanese platform responding to EU law has different edges from domestic Japanese regulation.
4. Record each claim with its source URL, locator, publication date, access date, original language, translated paraphrase, source class and unresolved verification issue.
5. Map layers should be selectable: **law/framework**, **operative sector duties**, **proposals/standards projects**, **pilots/platforms**, **international governance** and **export-market exposure**.
6. Preserve explicit uncertainty: `not_identified_in_review`, `official_text_not_retrieved`, `pending_secondary_verification`, and `planned_not_confirmed`. No evidence should never be rendered as a confident legal “no”.

Priority follow-up before manuscript claims: Vietnam mandatory rollout roadmap/decree text; China Article 12 measures; Korean consolidated laws and 2026 rules; official AIS-227; California final chapter text; Brazil corrected gazette annex; actual awards/outcomes for Canada and Egypt; additional Latin American, African and ASEAN jurisdictions.
