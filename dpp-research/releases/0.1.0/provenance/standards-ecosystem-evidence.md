# DPP standardisation and ecosystem evidence

Research cut-off: **23 October 2026**. Primary-source desk research. This register distinguishes laws, standards, standardisation projects, research outputs, industry implementations and certification schemes. Catalogue publication, European availability, national adoption and EU Official Journal citation are separate events.

## 1. European horizontal standards: eight published, six OJ-cited

CEN-CENELEC JTC 24 develops the cross-sector DPP framework and system under Commission request M/604 (amended by M/604 Amd 1). Product-specific data content is outside its horizontal scope. The published standards do not by themselves establish a product's passport obligation. [CEN-CENELEC explanation, 15 July 2026](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2026/en-in-the-spotlight/2026-07-15-dpp/).

**Legal event:** [Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1736](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32026D1736), adopted 14 July and published 15 July 2026, cites the first six. Under ESPR Article 41(2), conformity gives a presumption only for covered requirements of Articles 10 and 11. It is not a general certification of regulatory compliance. The [Commission harmonised-standards register](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/goods/european-standards/harmonised-standards/digital-product-passport-dpp_en) currently lists that decision alone. Its [DPP timeline](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en) schedules the remaining-two decision for **November 2026**, explicitly indicative.

Do not describe all eight as OJ-cited or describe the two September publications as drafts. Conversely, do not equate publication or a national adoption deadline with the effective date of a product passport obligation.

## 3. CIRPASS and CIRPASS-2

The [CIRPASS-2 project](https://cirpass2.eu/) succeeds the CIRPASS preparatory action (2022–2024). Current consortium: **49 partners, 13 lighthouse pilots, four value chains**: textiles, electrical/electronic equipment, tyres and construction. The project runs **May 2024–April 2027**, according to consortium member [Fraunhofer IZM](https://www.izm.fraunhofer.de/en/institut/projects/cirpass-2.html). It targets operational, scalable DPPs, B2B circular-business use cases, cross-pilot interoperability, standardisation input and SME uptake. This is an EU-funded implementation/research programme, not legislation or a certification authority.

The [pilot catalogue](https://cirpass2.eu/lighthouse-pilots/) includes circular workwear, connected apparel, mattresses, textile SMEs, vacuum-cleaner repairs, DPP4.0, audio/emergency lighting, IT reuse/end-of-life, tumble-dryer refurbishment and parts recovery, tyres and construction. Pilot presence should be mapped separately from national law or commercial market adoption.

DOI fetch failed; description verified from publisher index. |
| DPP Standardisation Initiatives & Projects around the world | [Project results](https://cirpass2.eu/project-results/), 25 June 2026 | Useful additional discovery inventory. It is selective and ongoing, not a complete global legal register. Full report not inspected. |
| DPP ontologies documentation | [CIRPASS-2 documentation](https://cirpass-2.github.io/ontologies-docs/), announced 12 October 2026 in publisher results | Particularly relevant for the proposed knowledge graph; explicitly exploratory, incomplete, non-normative and **not an official EU ontology**. |

Ontology design evidence: cross-sector CORE plus sector extensions; separate modules for products/passports, actors, identifiers, materials, substances, compliance, lifecycle events, LCA and connectors. Product profiles/templates and SHACL shapes express required content/cardinality separately from ontology semantics. It reuses external terms and requires source traceability. The battery extension draws on the Batteries Regulation and BatteryPass-Ready; the tyre ontology is an example rather than the future official tyre ontology. The textile network is v1.1.2. Competency questions have not yet been formally validated against product data. These caveats come from the [documentation itself](https://cirpass-2.github.io/ontologies-docs/).

**Research implication:** reuse and align relevant concepts rather than importing the entire ontology as legal truth. Our research graph needs additional classes for legal instruments, legal status, dated obligations, standards publication/citation, initiatives, jurisdiction and evidence. CIRPASS ontology models principally describe products/passports; they do not substitute for a global regulatory knowledge graph.

## 5. Proposed graph and map distinctions

These are research-design inferences, not normative claims:

- Model **StandardsBody**, **Committee**, **Standard**, **StandardVersion**, **StandardisationProject**, **LegalInstrument**, **CitationEvent**, **Initiative**, **Pilot**, **DataModel**, **Ontology**, **CertificationScheme**, **SoftwareComponent**, **Jurisdiction**, **Sector** and **SourceEvidence** separately.
- Useful edges: `developedBy`, `respondsToMandate`, `citedBy`, `presumesConformityWith`, `normativelyReferences`, `reusesVocabulary`, `implements`, `pilots`, `fundedBy`, `successorOf`, `targetsSector`, `operatesIn`, `hasPlannedMilestone`.
- Capture date type explicitly: adopted, ratified, published, available, nationally implemented, OJ-cited, legally applicable, project started, pilot demonstrated, certification planned.
- Map legal coverage independently from initiative headquarters, pilot locations and international scope. A European standards body is not an EU law; participation in an international project is not national DPP legislation.
- Keep conformance evidence at the level actually tested: interface, schema, identifier, access control, semantics, assurance or legal obligation. Interoperability claims should identify versions, profiles, test evidence and participating systems.

## 6. Remaining verification limits

1. Full CEN standard text was not reviewed; scope/status/catalogue and OJ are verified. No clause-level implementation advice is asserted. Exact CEN availability dates for EN 18222 and EN 18223 were not independently retrieved in this pass; national adoption dates were. [NEN's EN 18223 page](https://www.nen.nl/en/nen-en-18223-2026-en-352704) advertises free viewing through a NEN Connect account, useful for a later clause-level review.
2. Latest JTC 24 dictionary-referencing work-item stage needs a direct catalogue follow-up; DKE confirms ongoing work, not publication.
3. ISO project names changed/split; some summaries retain the earlier overview title for Part 1. The latest ISO catalogue should control status and naming.
4. CIRPASS D4.1 repository version v4 and report version V1.1 differ; record both. Full-text architecture comparison and AAS/RDF report analysis remain useful next research tasks.
5. GBA and consortium success claims should be paired with independent outcomes before asserting economic impact, complete cross-border interoperability or production adoption at scale.
