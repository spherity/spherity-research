# Candidate research protocol 0.2.0

Predecessor: immutable protocol 0.1.0. Candidate checked 25 October 2026. Existing extraction and evidence-access rules continue: primary_rechecked requires reopened primary material, while indexed excerpts and inherited dossiers retain their own limits. No source access is upgraded by a linked record.

Additions have unique IDs; meaning-changing corrections retain before/after records in change-manifest.json. Replaced assertions receive new IDs; old assertions remain marked superseded. All seven earlier website batches are retained intact. No live service transaction, EDC execution, independent interoperability reproduction or human adjudication was performed.

The calendar distinguishes publication, adoption, entry into force, application, indicative plans and conditional events. Adjacent Machinery/CRA duties are not DPP mandates. International AAS associations are navigation evidence, not national adoption. The UK outline is a locator; GB and NI scopes remain separate.
