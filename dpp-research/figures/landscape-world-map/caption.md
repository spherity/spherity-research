# Figure 1. Selected global DPP evidence coverage and transnational institutions

Panel A maps selected geographic coverage in research release **0.1.0**, with an evidence cut-off of **23 October 2026**. Seventeen of the release's 44 country rows contain at least one directly assigned record; 26 contain only EU-membership display joins. Germany has both direct records and EU joins and is coloured as direct coverage. The remaining country row, the United Kingdom, has no country-level record: Great Britain and Northern Ireland are represented separately. Grey combines geographies with no country-level extraction and those outside the selected review; it never means that DPP law, activity or capability is absent. Direct records can describe legal instruments, policy, standards, pilots or ecosystems and are not independent legal assessments. The 27 EU records repeated across member-country rows represent scope, not demonstrated national implementation. There are 128 unique substantive records across all geographic scopes; country-row counts must not be summed to estimate unique records.

Panel B presents illustrative institutions or artifacts with explicitly global, European or EU scope: the W3C DPP and Business Wallet Task Force, ISO/IEC JTC 5, UNTP's maintained 0.7.0 pilot release, the Global Battery Alliance programme, non-normative RAIN-DPP-001 guidance, CEN-CENELEC JTC 24 and CIRPASS-2. Their placement does not imply domestic legal obligations, universal adoption, completed standards, legal recognition or proven cross-system interoperability. These examples are deliberately not assigned to countries by headquarters or participation.

The main map uses an **Equal Earth** projection; the European detail uses Mercator for local legibility. Country polygons come from **Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries**, repository release **v5.1.2** (the project's layer page advertises layer 5.1.1), in the public domain. The 177 geometry features are not a count of recognised states or researched jurisdictions. Forty-two of the 44 country rows join to geometry; Malta and Singapore have dataset rows but are not resolved by this geometry. Subnational and territorial scope, including Great Britain, Northern Ireland, California, Paraná and Busan, is retained in the accompanying coverage table rather than inferred from national polygons. Natural Earth uses de facto boundaries; the figure does not endorse territorial claims. All substantive claims remain pending qualified human review.
