Seven separate assessment lenses and scope limits

ESPR / DPP

Can product identity, materials and lifecycle claims be traced? ESPR DPP duties depend on applicable product measures and dates; a humanoid does not automatically require an ESPR DPP.

Functional safety

Does accepted evidence support this configuration in this task? Functional safety is a discipline and assurance task. A single test result does not establish system safety.

EU AI Act

What is the AI function, intended use and applicable assurance route? Classification, duty holder and sectoral route require assessment; a robot or AI assistant is not automatically high-risk.

MDR · medical branch

Would a medical intended purpose change the required evidence? The base case is non-medical. A medical variant would require its own classification, clinical evidence and conformity route; these are not supplied here.

Machinery Regulation

Which risk assessment, technical documentation and conformity route apply? Check the placing-on-market date and applicable machinery regime. The figure does not grant conformity or CE marking.

Cyber Resilience Act

Which software, vulnerabilities, support duties and incidents matter? Determine product scope, exclusions and staged duties. An SBOM is useful evidence, not a complete CRA assurance case.

Export controls

What classification, destination, end user and end use must be checked? No automatic licence requirement follows from the word robot. List entries, catch-all provisions and other relevant restrictions need case-specific review.
